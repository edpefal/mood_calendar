Support

Need help with Mood Calendar? Write to support@moodcalendar.app and include your device model and app version (Settings → Version) so we can help you faster.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Store tab and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, from your Google Play order history.

The daily reminder does not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Mood Calendar in your system settings, and that the reminder is on in Settings.

How do I export or delete my data?

Your moods and notes live only on your device. Use Settings → Export history to save a copy. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

How do I stop sharing usage data?

Turn off “Share anonymous usage data” in Settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.
